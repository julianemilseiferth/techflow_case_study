Improving Customer Support Wait Times

TechFlow Solutions Case Study

Business Problem

Customer pain

Customers calling during peak business hours face long waits, creating frustration, lost productivity, and reputational risk.

Management gap

Leaders suspect Monday and Friday demand spikes, but they lack structured historical data to justify staffing changes.

Data and Method

🧪

Simulation

Generated 1,200 call records with Faker, random, NumPy, and datetime-based logic.

🗄️

Storage

Stored records in SQLite using SQLAlchemy for reproducible analysis and Streamlit display.

📊

Outputs

Exported CSV data and built a dashboard to inspect day-level and hour-level support patterns.

Recommendation

Staffing action

Increase support staffing by about 40% on Monday and Friday from 1 PM to 5 PM, then add lighter coverage around the other busiest hours.

Operational support

Add callback options, self-service billing/account resources, and live monitoring so spikes can be handled before queues grow too long.
